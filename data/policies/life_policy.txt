Life Insurance Policy

Section 1: Death Benefit
The policy provides a death benefit to the nominated beneficiary
if the insured person dies during the active policy term,
subject to the policy terms.

Section 2: Premium Payments
The policyholder must pay premiums according to the selected
payment schedule to keep the policy active.

Section 3: Exclusions
The policy does not cover claims arising from exclusions
specified in the policy contract.
