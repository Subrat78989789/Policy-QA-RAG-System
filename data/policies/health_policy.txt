Health Insurance Policy

Section 1: Waiting Period
The policy has a 30-day initial waiting period for illnesses.
Accidental injuries are covered from the policy start date.

Section 2: Hospitalization
The policy covers eligible hospitalization expenses when
the insured person is admitted for at least 24 consecutive hours.

Section 3: Exclusions
Cosmetic procedures are not covered unless medically necessary
due to an accident or illness.
