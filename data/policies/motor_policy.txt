Motor Insurance Policy

Section 1: Own Damage
The policy covers accidental damage to the insured vehicle,
including damage caused by collisions, fire, and natural disasters,
subject to the policy terms.

Section 2: Third-Party Liability
The policy covers eligible legal liability for injury or property
damage caused to a third party by the insured vehicle.

Section 3: Exclusions
Damage caused while driving without a valid driving licence
is excluded from coverage.
